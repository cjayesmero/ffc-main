Operational Guides

I. Electrical Wiring Connection
Step 1: In the top section of the device, lift the top cover of the control box. Step 2: Carefully place the batteries on the battery holders. Step 3: Attach the two propellers to each designated connector. Step 4: Turn on the three switches located inside and outside of the control box. Step 5: Close the control box.
II. Operating the Device
Step 1: Turn on Bluetooth in the phone settings. Step 2: Check the “ available devices” on the Bluetooth. Choose the device name “HC-05”. Step 3: Enter the passcode (1234) and pair. Step 4: Open the app and go to the “ Bluetooth devices”. Step 5: Find the name of the device “ HC-05” and Connect. Step 6: Check the indicator or listen to the sound. If the light turns on, it means it's connected. If not, go back to step 1.
